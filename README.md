# News & Election Tracker (India)

An intelligence platform that tracks and curates news for **school education, higher
education and skill development** in India, along with officer **transfer orders**,
**elections** down to the municipal level, **Supreme/High Court decisions** and
**policy decisions and statements**. Items are tagged by **state** and **sector** and
feed daily and weekly reports and a dashboard.

Constraints: running cost under **₹500/month**, 3–5 concurrent users, a mobile-friendly
training mode, and room to add sectors (such as healthcare), states and geographies
later.

> Status: **M0 — planning.** See the [Scope of Work](docs/SOW.md).
