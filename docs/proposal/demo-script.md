# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

Record the screen at 1080p with a voice-over. Rehearse once so each scene takes about the time shown.

**Before recording:**
- Run `python -m tracker run` and `python -m tracker report daily`.
- Open the files below in your browser.
- Have your phone ready with the bot, once `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` is set.

| # | Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:20 | Executive brief (`output/reports/daily-<date>/executive-brief.html`) | "Every morning at 7:30 this brief lands in email and on Telegram: the top developments in school, higher education and skills, plus transfers, elections and court rulings, across India." |
| 2 | 0:20–0:45 | Terminal: `python -m tracker run` scrolling | "Every two hours the tracker reads 54 news feeds in 9 languages and watches 8 government notice pages: UGC, CBSE, NTA, DoPT, the Supreme Court. This run: about 2,000 items in under a minute." |
| 3 | 0:45–1:15 | Dashboard, Feed tab; open one "Why this score" | "Each item is tagged by sector, state and category, and scored 0 to 100. The score is explainable. Here it's a court ruling on schools, in Maharashtra, because the Bombay High Court was named." |
| 4 | 1:15–1:35 | Filters: State = Maharashtra, then the Transfers tab | "Filter by state, sector or category. The Transfers tab lists officer postings: who moved where." |
| 5 | 1:35–1:55 | Insights tab | "Insights show where the news is by state, sector and language, and which layer decided each score." |
| 6 | 1:55–2:25 | Phone: Telegram cards, tap 👍 and 👎; forward a link | "The client trains it from his phone: 👍 relevant, 👎 not. Forward any link or PDF and it's added. Tonight the model retrains on these taps." |
| 7 | 2:25–2:45 | Excel Summary sheet (state × sector) | "The weekly Excel has a state-by-sector pivot and a sheet each for transfers, elections, courts and policy." |
| 8 | 2:45–3:00 | Dashboard, Run log tab | "It runs on free tiers, zero rupees a month, and every run is logged: the evidence for the two-week reliability run." |

**Closing card:** News & Election Tracker · live since 28 Sep 2026 · ₹0–150/month · Harshit Singh
